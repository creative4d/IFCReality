# IFCReality
**IFCReality** is a research project focused on developing and testing advanced methodologies for the recognition and semantic coding of architectural elements, following the ontology used in Building Information Modeling (**BIM**).
Using techniques from **computational geometry**, **computer vision**, and **artificial intelligence**, IFCReality processes point clouds to identify, segment, and reconstruct architectural components based on the **IFC data schema**.

---
Project Goals

- Recognize architectural elements from unstructured 3D data.
- Encode them according to the IFC semantic structure.
- Develop computational workflows for specific geometric and semantic patterns.
- Integrate geometric data with metadata (e.g. materials, functions, historical context).

---
Technologies & Methods

- **Computer Vision**: Segmentation and classification of point clouds.
- **Computational Geometry**: Extraction of generatrices and geometric directrices.
- **AI & Machine Learning**: Interpreting form and spatial relationships.
- **IFC Data Modeling**: Encoding and structuring data to fit BIM standards.
- **Iterative Validation**: Comparison and refinement using ground-truth data.

---
Current Focus

The team is currently working on a set of case studies to explore domain-specific workflows, focusing particularly on:
- Columns  
- Walls  
- Floors  

Each study is designed to extract the computational flow that best suits the element’s geometric characteristics and semantic roles.

---
Validation and Quality Control

A key part of the research is the **validation phase**, where the reconstructed elements are compared against reliable reference data:
- Deviation analysis and spatial alignment  
- Surface and structural conformity checks  
- Functional and organizational verification using semantic metadata  
- Iterative refinement for improving geometric and semantic accuracy  

---
Applications

IFCReality contributes to several areas of the AEC industry and heritage research:
- Architectural documentation and restoration  
- Automated modeling of existing buildings  
- Semantic enrichment for BIM-based design and facility management  
- Support for historical and structural analysis
